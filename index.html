Key Findings

74%
Female Survival Rate
Women survived at nearly 4× the rate of men, reflecting "women and children first" evacuation protocol followed by the crew.
62%
1st Class Survival
First class had the highest survival rate. Proximity to lifeboats on upper decks and priority access made a critical difference.
512
Max Fare Paid (£)
The highest fare recorded — over 170× the average fare of £3 — paid by a first class passenger.
29
Average Age
The average passenger was 29 years old. Missing ages were imputed with the mean for clean analysis.
S
Top Port: Southampton
Southampton (S) had the most passengers, followed by Cherbourg (C) and Queenstown (Q).
19%
Male Survival Rate
Only 1 in 5 male passengers survived. Within that, class still mattered — 1st class men fared far better than 3rd class men.
